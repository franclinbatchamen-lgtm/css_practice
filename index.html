🌞 Photosynthesis

One of the most important things plants do is photosynthesis. Plants use sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide to produce their own food in the form of glucose. During this process, they release oxygen into the atmosphere.

🍎 Plants as a Source of Food

Plants provide food for humans and animals all around the world. Fruits such as apples, bananas, oranges, and strawberries come from plants. Vegetables such as carrots, spinach, potatoes, tomatoes, and onions are also plant-based foods.

🌻 How Plants Respond to Their Environment

Plants have various ways of responding to their environment. For example, they can grow toward light (phototropism), close their leaves at night (nyctinasty), or change their root structure based on soil conditions.
